**Seribo™: Silk Microfiber Reinforced Polymer Composite**

Seribo™ is an advanced bioactive and bioresorbable composite, combining silk fibroin microfibers with polycaprolactone (PCL). This natural fibre–reinforced polymer composite delivers a unique balance of:

1. Bioactivity & Biomimicry  
2. Mechanical Strength & Physiological Bioresorption  
3. 3D Printability  
4. Sustainability  
5. Radiolucency

**Key Features & Advantages**

1\. Bioactivity & Biomimicry

* Silk Fibroin Bioactivity: Facilitates mesenchymal stem cell differentiation, enhances adhesion, and promotes osteogenesis.  
* Hydroxyapatite Nucleation: Serves as a natural template for bone mineral growth, improving osteointegration.  
* RGD Motif Advantage: Antheraea mylitta silk fibroin contains the RGD (Arg-Gly-Asp) sequence (GenBank: AY136274.1), which supports osteointegration, wound healing, and tissue regeneration.  
* Biomimetic Properties: Similar to collagen, Silk Fibroin promotes angiogenesis, collagen synthesis, and fracture healing. Carbonyl groups serve as nucleation sites for calcium salts, forming bone-like apatite with Ca/P ratios close to native bone.

2\. Physiological Bioresorption & Optimal Strength

* Controlled Resorption: Degrades at clinically relevant rates, synchronizing with natural healing.  
* No Secondary Surgery: Fully resorbable, eliminating implant removal.  
* Enhanced Mechanical Strength: \~1.5× stronger than pure PCL, offering reliable fixation.  
* Dynamic Support: Maintains stability while gradually transferring load to regenerating bone.

3\. 3D Printability

* Compatible with advanced 3D printing technologies.  
* Enables patient-specific implants and complex scaffold designs.  
* Interconnected porous structures promote: Cellular attachment and differentiation, Vascularisation and nutrient flow, Uniform tissue integration for faster bone regeneration

4\. Sustainability

* Locally sourced silk reduces environmental impact and strengthens indigenous value chains.  
* Green fabrication with 3D printing minimizes waste and ensures precision.  
* Commitment to eco-conscious, affordable healthcare, blending innovation with sustainability.

5\. Radiolucency

* Radiographic Transparency: Allows clear X-ray/CT imaging of underlying bone.  
* Enables accurate monitoring of healing and regeneration, unlike radiopaque metal implants.

**Seribo™ Scaffold System**

The Seribo™ Scaffold System is designed for bone regeneration in trauma, tumor, and defect management. Combining silk fibroin bioactivity with PCL’s resorbability, the system restores bone through osteoconductive and bioactive 3D printed scaffolds.

Available Formats

1. Patient-Specific Implants (PSI)  
2. Porous Bone Grafts  
3. Porous Bone Membranes

Difference Between PSI and Grafts/Membranes

* Patient-Specific Implants (PSI): Fully customized for each patient and defect, designed and 3D printed based on individual CT data to ensure precise anatomical fit.  
* Porous Bone Grafts & Membranes: Bulk-manufactured using 3D printing technology, providing standardized sizes and architectures suitable for a range of clinical applications.

1\. **Patient-Specific Implants (PSI)**

Our bioactive Seribo™ material can be 3D printed into Patient-Specific Implants (PSI), precisely tailored to match individual defects. The porous architecture, enriched with naturally occurring RGD sequences in silk microfibers, promotes osteoblast attachment, bone ingrowth, and regeneration, creating an ideal environment for healing. PSI enables more efficient surgeries by providing a precise fit that eliminates intraoperative implant modifications. This reduces wound exposure, minimizes operating time, and contributes to faster recovery and improved patient outcomes.

**Patient-Specific Implant (PSI) Workflow with Seribo™**

Our PSI implants are manufactured using FDM-based 3D printing technology with our patented Seribo™ filament, ensuring bioactivity, porosity, and bioresorbability tailored to each patient. The process involves several key steps:

* Patient Imaging: High-resolution CT scans of the defect site are acquired.  
* 3D Reconstruction: CT data is processed to generate an accurate 3D model of the patient’s anatomy.  
* Implant Design: Using advanced CAD software, the implant is designed to perfectly fit the patient’s defect while incorporating optimal porosity for bone ingrowth.  
* 3D Printing: The customized implant is fabricated using FDM 3D printing with Seribo™, producing a bioactive, porous, and bioresorbable scaffold.  
* Post-Processing & Sterilization: Printed implants undergo finishing and sterilization procedures to ensure surgical readiness.  
* Surgical Placement: The PSI is implanted with a precise fit, reducing intraoperative modifications, minimizing wound exposure, shortening operating time, and supporting faster bone regeneration.

2\. **Porous Bone Grafts**

Seribo™ porous bone grafts are designed as bioactive, osteoconductive scaffolds to support bone regeneration in a variety of clinical scenarios. Their interconnected porous architecture facilitates cellular infiltration, vascularization, and natural bone remodelling, while providing a stable framework for autografts or bone substitutes.

Key Features:

* Osteoconductive: Guides new bone formation within defect sites.  
* Porous Network: Promotes vascularization, cell infiltration, and bone remodelling.  
* Bioresorbable: Gradually replaced by the patient’s native bone.   
* Versatile: Suitable for tumor reconstruction, large bone defects, and defect filling in mandibular and orthopedic applications.

Mechanistic Advantage:

* Porosity allows bone ingrowth and vascularization, enhancing natural healing.  
* Acts as a carrier for autografts, providing structural support in larger defects.  
* Innate bioactivity of silk fibroin enhances osteoblast attachment and proliferation.  
* Can be used in combination with titanium plates and screws in load-bearing regions, where the graft supports bone regeneration and the fixation devices provide mechanical stability.

Clinical Applications:

* Mandibular reconstruction with autograft support  
* Large segmental bone defects in orthopedic or craniofacial surgery  
* Tumor-related bone defects  
* Load-bearing reconstructions, when combined with rigid fixation 

3\. **Porous Bone Membranes**

Seribo™ porous membranes are designed for predictable bone regeneration across a broad range of clinical scenarios, leveraging their bioactive, porous architecture to enhance cellular response and osteogenesis.

**Dental Applications:**

Its adaptable structure allows quick and easy contouring during surgery, supporting bone healing in alveolar socket repair and residual ridge preservation after tooth extraction.

These membranes are engineered to:

•	Preserve and enhance bone height and width for future implant placement

•	Support natural healing and resorb completely within 6–9 months ( The duration of the resorption process depends on the size, thickness, and porosity of the membrane.)

•	Offer dual functionality with a smooth outer layer to prevent epithelial proliferation and porous inner layers that promote osteoblast attachment for guided bone regeneration.

Applications:

1. Extraction Socket Preservation: Maintains alveolar bone volume after tooth extraction.  
2. Localized Ridge Augmentation: Supports site-specific bone regeneration for implant readiness.  
3. Alveolar Ridge Reconstruction: Facilitates restoration of bone height and width in compromised ridges.  
4. Guided Bone Regeneration (GBR) in Dehiscence Defects: Promotes bone regeneration while preventing soft tissue intrusion.  
5. GBR in Periodontal Defects: Encourages osteoblast attachment and mineralised matrix formation.

**Craniofacial & Orthopedic Applications:** 

1. Comminuted fractures: In fractures with multiple fracture fragments, membrane is utilized to hold the bone fragments together, maintaining spatial orientation and promoting coordinated healing.  
2. Load-Bearing Applications: When used in combination with rigid fixation devices such as titanium plates and screws, Seribo™ membranes enhance osteogenic cellular activity, while the metal constructs provide mechanical stability under functional loads.  
3. Mechanistic Advantage: Seribo™ membranes act as a bioactive scaffold, facilitating early cellular adhesion, proliferation, and differentiation. This synergy between the membrane’s osteoconductive properties and mechanical fixation accelerates bone regeneration in both non-load-bearing and mechanically demanding environments.

⚠️ Important: Adequate soft tissue coverage is required for membrane placement. Use is contraindicated if soft tissue closure is not achievable.

**Seribo™ Bioresorbable Fixation System — CMF**

Seribo™ offers a bioresorbable fixation system for the stabilisation of fractures and reconstruction of the craniomaxillofacial skeleton.  These implants provide fixation during bone healing, then resorb naturally, avoiding removal surgery.

Especially beneficial in pediatric cases, where ongoing bone growth makes permanent metal implants unsuitable.

Indications

* Fracture repair and reconstruction in the craniomaxillofacial skeleton (pediatric & adult).  
* Non-load-bearing use in alignment and fixation of fragments, grafts, or substitutes in CMF and mandibular regions.

Contraindications

* Areas with active/latent infection.  
* Sites with insufficient bone quality, quantity, or vascular supply.

Plate Design – Features & Benefits

1. Beveled Edge: Reduces palpability for improved patient comfort.  
2. Partial Countersinks: Provide stable plate–screw interfaces, minimize micromotion, and maintain low profile.  
3. Straight-Edged Design: Maintains plate thickness at boundaries for superior strength and resistance to deformation.

(Available in CMF Plates, Orbital Plates, Neuro Plates, Mesh Plates)
